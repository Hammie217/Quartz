My career has taken me from hands-on engineering and automation projects into leading digital teams and influencing architecture across global manufacturing operations.

I'm particularly interested in the practical challenges of modernising industrial technology: adopting better software engineering practices, making manufacturing data useful, and building capabilities that scale beyond individual projects.

## A career built on practical experience

### 01 / Engineering Foundation

Working with automation and capital projects developed my understanding of industrial systems, technical implementation and the responsibility that comes with delivery.

### 02 / Digital Implementation

Moving into digitalisation meant connecting technology with operational process improvement, not just implementing another system.

### 03 / Leadership and Strategy

Leading a digital team brought a broader focus: prioritisation, governance, stakeholder alignment and developing capability in others.

### 04 / Global Architecture and Integration

My current focus is global OT data integration, industrial connectivity standards and architecture collaboration across manufacturing operations.

## Questions that keep me learning

**The future of industrial software.** How can manufacturing adopt better software engineering and deployment practices without losing sight of operational reliability?

**Meaningful use of manufacturing data.** How do we make data understandable, connected and useful beyond the system that generated it?

**Developing the industry through knowledge sharing.** How can we make practical engineering knowledge more accessible and help others build on it?
