This move starts and finishes in [[Open hold]] or [[Closed hold]]
### How to lead
To lead the mambo step, use the leads left hand and guide hand as it rests in leads, lightly towards the follower. Generally this will continue until another step is led
### The move
A simple forward and back

2 - Right foot tap in position
