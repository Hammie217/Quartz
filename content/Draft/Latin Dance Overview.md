I've recently started taking dance lessons for Bachata and Salsa, I find it great fun but struggle with the social dancing at the end where the pre-determined plan goes out the window and it's all up to my shoddy memory and leading style to keep the dance moving. In an attempt to improve my memory and also understanding of these moves; my intent is to document these moves hopefully acting as a reminder, guidebook and also force me to actually understand the steps well enough to write about them.

With both of Bachata and Salsa there are multiple holds which are swapped between depending on the moves executed. This generates a sort of state diagram in my head where we move between these hold states via move vectors, I'm currently resisting every fibre of my being trying to turn dance into a digital state diagram but maybe in the future.

For now the basic holds in my repertoire are:
## Salsa
- Basic hold - Leaders right hand on followers shoulder blade. Leaders left hand holding followers right
- Open hold - Followers hands laid in leaders
