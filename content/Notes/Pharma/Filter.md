A filter is a device used to separate particles from a liquid or gas, for example in [[HVAC]] for graded environments a series of filters ([[Bag Filter]], [[HEPA]]) are used to remove particulates and microbes from the air.

Filters are commonly used for sterilisation of services or product, for example a system that requires sterile nitrogen could use 

In high criticality situations, double filtration is commonly used to:
1. improve performance of the filtration
2. Reduce risk of filter failure causing a batch/campaign write-off

Filters capture particulates through four main mechanisms:
- [[Impaction]]
- [[Interception]]
- [[Diffusion]]
- [[Electrostatic attraction]] - Only for charged particles

Filters typically define a [[MPPS]] value and what percentage of [[MPPS]] particulates are filtered.
