A pressure cascade is a design of clean room [[HVAC]] where areas with higher grades are kept at a higher pressure than lower graded areas. 

This pressure difference ensures clean air flows into dirty areas instead of dirty air into clean areas preventing dust ingress.

A pressure cascade is required to be monitored by some [[Environmental Monitoring System]] to prove compliance and alert to excursions.

To prevent large tubulance and backdraft in a room when a door opens between a pressure cascade, [[Pressure Relief Damper]]s are commonly used.
