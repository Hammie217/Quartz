Terminal Sterilisation is the process of [[Sterilisation]] of a product after the [[Manufacturing]] and [[Filling]] process has been completed, [[Syringes]] or [[Vials]] are then typically passed through [[Autoclave]] sterilising the product in place. This means that the sterile boundary is never open to the environment as it is sterilised in place. This is a similar process to how tinned food is cooked after being placed in the can.

As bacteria can enter the product downstream, Terminal sterilisation has strict [[BioBurden]] requirements to ensure [[Sterilisation]] and breakdown of [[Endotoxins]].

Terminal Sterilisation is a practice used where possible for [[Sterilisation]] of product due to the simplicity and intrinsic safety.
