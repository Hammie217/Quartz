(Continuous) Particulate Monitoring, or CPM, is a part of required [[Environmental Monitoring System]] for area classification. Graded areas come with requirements on the size of particles allowed, amount of particles allowed for each size and the monitoring requirements of the area (Continuous or periodic).

Fixed [[Isokinetic probes]] are typically installed in critical areas such as within an [[Isolator]] or [[RABS]] such as in [[Filling]] or at [[Autoclave]] exist. These fixed probes can run throughout full manufacturing cycles alerting if limits are hit in real time.

Mobile [[Isokinetic probes]] are commonly used in less critical or lower graded areas for routine testing or testing airflow post room or equipment modification.
