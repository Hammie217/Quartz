A downflow booth (Commonly called a Dispensing booth, when used for dispensing) is a piece of equipment usually a few metres squared in size that produces an area under top-down [[UDAF]]. A downflow booth is typically in a [[Manufacturing]] process, for a couple of purposes
1. Protect the product when vessels (Or any [[Sanitary]] container) are opened 
2. Protect the operator from ingesting harmful particles such as [[Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient]] during addition.

As downflow booths are commonly used for handling powders, it is a common requirement for these to be [[ATEX]] rated.
