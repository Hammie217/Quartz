A Building Management System, or BMS, is a control system implemented commonly in both manufacturing and office spaces to control building services such as [[HVAC]], lighting and power. 

BMS is not typically a quality system, but the same technology can be implemented in a validated environment called a [[Environmental Monitoring System]].
