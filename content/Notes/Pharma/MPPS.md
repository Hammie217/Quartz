Most Penetrating Particle Size (MPPS) is a key metric used to determine a [[Filter]]'s efficiency, it defines the worst case size of a particle for that filter design. As filters make use of four main mechanisms:
- [[Impaction]]
- [[Interception]]
- [[Diffusion]]
- [[Electrostatic attraction]] - Only for charged particles

they vary in effectiveness based on the mechanism in play, typically in the [[Interception]] range where a particle is too large for [[Impaction]] and too small for [[Diffusion]].
